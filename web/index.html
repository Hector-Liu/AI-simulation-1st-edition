Why is every prompt checked for banned words?

The study asks whether a shared label emerges from pairwise interaction alone. Words such as agree, coordinate, consensus, majority, most, the group would tell agents what to do or reveal group-level information, and reward words (points, score, win) would leak an incentive into the no-reward arm. Before a prompt is sent, it is scanned; any hit stops the run and is written to leakage_report.json.

What makes a good label set?
          
Use short made-up words with the same shape (for example consonant-vowel-consonant-vowel). Avoid real words in any language, names, brands and pairs that differ by one letter: these create preferences that have nothing to do with interaction. Errors (duplicates, spaces, banned strings) block saving; warnings are for your judgment.
